Patent filedSmart India Hackathon 2026

StigRon: an AI-powered electrostatic pollination rover

An autonomous rover that finds flowers ready for pollination, drives along crop rows, and transfers pollen between chosen plants for controlled cross-pollination. I co-developed it with my team.

YOLO · LiDAR · IMU · RTK-GPS · 3-axis robotic arm · electrostatic collector

  • Built and integrated the hardware: tracked drive, 3-axis robotic arm, electrostatic pollen collector with an air-resistance disk, and LiDAR, IMU and RTK-GPS modules.
  • Worked on YOLO-based flower and bloom-stage detection, with a camera check after every pollen transfer.
  • Contributed to the companion app that plans crosses and logs breeding data.
Drone software · NIDAR 2027
